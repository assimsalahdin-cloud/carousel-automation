Privacy Policy

Carousel Automation · Effective date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Carousel Automation processes information when a user connects a supported social media account and publishes approved carousel content.

1. Information processed

Depending on the connected platform and permissions granted, the service may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, content selected for publication, image URLs, captions, scheduling information, and publication status or post identifiers.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate connected accounts, prepare approved content, publish that content through official platform APIs, prevent duplicate publishing, and record publication status.

3. Data sharing

Information is shared only with the third-party platforms and service providers necessary to perform the requested workflow. Personal information is not sold to advertisers.

4. Data retention

Operational data may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to run the workflow, troubleshoot errors, and prevent duplicate publication. Authorization credentials should be stored securely in the workflow platform's credential system.

5. Security

Reasonable technical measures should be used to protect credentials and workflow data. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may revoke a connected platform's authorization through that platform's account or app settings. Revoking authorization may prevent Carousel Automation from publishing further content.

7. Third-party services

TikTok, Instagram, and other connected services operate under their own privacy policies. Carousel Automation does not control their independent data practices.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the workflow, connected services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
